YOUR BEGINNER JOURNEY

Four milestones. One clear path.

Progress is not measured by tricks. It is measured by control, safety and the point where you can make good decisions on your own.

01

Control

Wind window, setup, launch and landing. Learn to move the kite intentionally before entering the water.

SHORE • 1–2 H
02

Body drag

Use the kite to move through the water without a board, recover equipment and stay oriented.

WATER • 1–2 H
03

Water start

Coordinate kite power, board angle and body position until the first controlled rides begin to happen.

WATER • 2–4 H
04

Ride independent

Build repeatability, ride both directions and learn the decisions that matter when an instructor steps back.

CAN I LEARN?

Probably yes. Start with three honest questions.

Do I need to be very strong?

No. Technique and kite control matter more than brute strength. Normal general fitness is enough for a first lesson.

Do I need surfing experience?

No. Complete beginners are expected. Board skills come after safety and kite control.

What if there is no wind?

Wind is part of the sport. A real school needs a rescheduling policy and lesson windows rather than pretending every slot is guaranteed.

BEGINNER FAQ

Before you get wet.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, towel, water and sunscreen. The demo school provides technical equipment and safety gear.

How many lessons until I ride?

There is no honest fixed number. Conditions, coordination and lesson format matter. The 6-hour beginner package is designed to reach the water-start phase, not promise independence.

Is the booking a real reservation?

No. This is a portfolio theme and the booking flow is a working front-end prototype. It deliberately does not charge money or claim real availability.
