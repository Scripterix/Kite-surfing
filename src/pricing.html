WHAT IS INCLUDED

Bring yourself. We handle the technical kit.

Lesson equipment

Kite, bar, board, harness, helmet and radio communication are presented as included in the theme package. Sizes and exact equipment would be handled by a real school.

Wind policy

The schedule should be treated as a lesson window. If wind conditions are unsuitable, a real implementation would move the booking rather than force the session.

GEAR WITHOUT THE SHOPPING LIST

Your first decision should be a lesson, not a kite purchase.

Beginners benefit from equipment matched to wind strength, rider size and lesson stage. Buying too early often creates more questions than progress.
